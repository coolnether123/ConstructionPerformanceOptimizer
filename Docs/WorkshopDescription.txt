[h1]Construction Pipeline Optimizer[/h1]

A focused RimWorld construction performance mod for active construction jobs and the zero-cost terrain pipeline used by Tilled Soil and similar mods.

[h2]What it optimizes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Continues a completed no-cost frame directly into the vanilla FinishFrame job instead of returning immediately to global work selection.
[*]Asks the active construction work giver for the next job, preserving mod-supplied replacement and prerequisite jobs.
[*]Distributes every active finish job's repeated defensive validation over a deterministic cadence when the exact vanilla hook has no direct Harmony conflict.
[*]Preserves a final vanilla construction authorization immediately before the frame completes.
[*]Fails closed: the cadence and completion guard disable as a pair if compatibility or exact IL checks fail.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Harmony.
[*]Supports RimWorld 1.6.
[*]Tilled Soil is supported but not required.
[*]No settings and no save data; normally safe to add or remove between sessions. Keep a normal save backup when changing mods.
[*]Multiplayer compatibility is expected but has not been verified in a two-client session. Matching versions and load order are required for testing, not a support claim.
[*][b]Do not enable this with Construction Queue Optimizer.[/b] The legacy package contains the same patches.
[/list]

[h2]Scope[/h2]
This is intentionally not a broad performance overhaul. It contains no turret, pawn-health, refuelable, dissolution, mechanitor, rendering, or general tick patches. That narrow scope reduces overlap with established performance mods.

[h2]Performance evidence[/h2]
On a derived save with exactly 12,000 Tilled Soil blueprints, the exact committed 1.6 DLL completed nine fresh-process 60-second samples per arm in three randomized blocks. Adaptive measured 124.18 mean / 115.31 median TPS versus baseline 114.19 mean / 111.11 median TPS: +8.75% by mean and +3.78% by median. Block-mean gains were +7.10%, +18.84%, and +0.11%, and 93.357% of repeated validation opportunities were skipped. The 95% confidence intervals overlap, so the positive pooled result is not statistically established. This is an extreme workload observation, not a guarantee for ordinary colonies; Multiplayer remains unverified without a two-client test.

[h2]Reporting problems[/h2]
Include your complete mod list, player log, RimWorld version, whether Multiplayer is active, and a reproducible construction example. Keep a save backup before changing a large mod list.
