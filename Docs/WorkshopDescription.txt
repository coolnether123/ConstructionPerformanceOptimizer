[h1]Construction Performance Optimizer[/h1]

A focused RimWorld construction performance mod for active construction jobs and the zero-cost terrain pipeline used by Tilled Soil and similar mods.

[b]Helps RimWorld stay responsive when your colony has a large construction backlog.[/b]

Colonies with hundreds or thousands of blueprints can spend a surprising amount of processing time repeatedly checking unfinished construction. CPO reduces that unnecessary work so the game can spend more time advancing the simulation.

You are most likely to notice the benefit with large building plans, long construction queues, many builders, higher game speeds, or terrain-building mods such as Tilled Soil. The improvement depends on how much construction is active and may be barely noticeable when little is being built.

CPO improves game performance. It does not make pawns build faster, change work priorities, skip material delivery, alter construction success chances, or bypass final construction authorization.

[h2]What it optimizes[/h2]
[list]
[*]Continues a completed no-cost frame directly into the vanilla FinishFrame job instead of returning immediately to global work selection.
[*]Asks the active construction work giver for the next job, preserving mod-supplied replacement and prerequisite jobs.
[*]On RimWorld 1.1-1.6, distributes every active finish job's repeated defensive validation over a deterministic cadence when the exact vanilla hook has no direct Harmony conflict.
[*]Preserves a final vanilla construction authorization immediately before the frame completes.
[*]Fails closed: the cadence and completion guard disable as a pair if compatibility or exact IL checks fail.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires Harmony.
[*]Supports RimWorld 1.0 through 1.6 in the main package.
[*]Tilled Soil is supported but not required.
[*]No settings and no save data; normally safe to add or remove between sessions. Keep a normal save backup when changing mods.
[*]RimWorld 1.5 and 1.6 Multiplayer are supported. Older versions are single-player only. Every player must use the same CPO version and mod list.
[*][b]Do not enable this with Construction Queue Optimizer.[/b] The legacy package contains the same patches.
[/list]

[h2]Scope[/h2]
This is intentionally not a broad performance overhaul. It contains no turret, pawn-health, refuelable, dissolution, mechanitor, rendering, or general tick patches. That narrow scope reduces overlap with established performance mods.

[h2]Performance evidence[/h2]
Controlled testing with a very large construction backlog confirmed improved simulation throughput and less processing work per game tick. Results vary with colony state, hardware, mod list, and active construction, so CPO does not promise a fixed gain for ordinary colonies. The largest benefits are expected when repeated construction checks or zero-cost terrain job searches are a meaningful bottleneck.

[h2]Reporting problems[/h2]
Include your complete mod list, player log, RimWorld version, whether Multiplayer is active, and a reproducible construction example. Keep a save backup before changing a large mod list.
